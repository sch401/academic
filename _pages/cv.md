## Mixing and mass transfer of biomass particles

**September 2023 – June 2025**

I investigated the drawdown and suspension of corn stover in stirred tanks containing a non Newtonian fluid. Image recognition was used to quantify particle behaviour. Coupled computational fluid dynamics and discrete element modelling revealed how mixing conditions affect the drawdown and suspension of floating particles.

I also developed a method for measuring mass transfer between porous floating particles and the surrounding liquid during mixing, and examined how chemical pretreatment changes these processes.

## Biomass data and methane yield prediction

**September 2021 – June 2023**

I constructed a database linking the physicochemical properties of lignocellulosic wastes with digestion conditions and methane yields. I developed machine learning models to predict biomethane production from untreated and chemically pretreated feedstocks.

The [BCBPD database](https://bcbpd.top) contains over 15,000 data entries covering 157 untreated and 11 pretreated lignocellulosic wastes, and provides access to the data and predictive tools.

## Pretreatment for anaerobic digestion

**September 2019 – June 2021**

I compared methane production from vinegar residue under liquid and solid state conditions. I also studied how anaerobic and microaerobic pretreatment affect methane production and microbial community development during the digestion of paper waste.
