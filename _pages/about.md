I am Chao Song, a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the **University of Manchester**. My research examines how biomass properties, pretreatment and mixing affect anaerobic digestion and methane production.

I combine experiments, computational modelling and machine learning to study the conversion of organic waste into useful resources. I received my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from Beijing University of Chemical Technology in June 2025 and joined Manchester in April 2026.

## Research interests

* **Biomass conversion and anaerobic digestion:** understanding how feedstock properties and pretreatment influence methane production.
* **Machine learning for bioenergy:** developing predictive models and curating data on lignocellulosic wastes.
* **Multiphase mixing and mass transfer:** studying the drawdown, suspension and mass transfer of floating biomass particles in stirred tanks.

## Database and tools

I developed the [Biomass Characteristics and Biomethane Production Database (BCBPD)](https://bcbpd.top), which provides access to biomass data and methane yield prediction tools. The database contains over **15,000 data entries**, covering **157 untreated and 11 pretreated lignocellulosic wastes**.
